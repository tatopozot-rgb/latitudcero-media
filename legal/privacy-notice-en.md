**Controller:** Renato {{surnames}} (Latitud Cero Studio), {{city}}, Ecuador. Email: latitudcerostudio@gmail.com.

**What we collect:** what you write in our forms (name, email, country, plan, idea or message) and what you send us by email or WhatsApp. If you hire us, we also collect your billing and payment details (PayPal or bank transfer). We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

**Why we use it:**
- To reply to you and send your proposal. Legal basis: your request and steps prior to a contract.
- To build your site, charge you and issue invoices. Legal basis: the contract and our tax obligations.

We do not send you marketing unless you ask for it.

**How long we keep it:**
- Requests that do not become a contract: up to 12 months.
- Client data: for as long as the service lasts, then for the period tax law requires (up to 7 years for invoices).

**Who we share it with:** only the providers we need to operate: {{site and form hosting}}, Google (email), Meta (WhatsApp), PayPal (payments) and, if your site runs on Wix or Shopify, those platforms. Some of them are outside Ecuador, for example in the US, and protect data under their own contractual safeguards. We never sell data.

**Your visitors' data:** if your site collects data from your visitors (bookings, forms), you are the controller. We act only as your processor: we use that data only to provide our service, we do not share it, and we delete it when the service ends.

**Your rights:** you can ask to access, correct, delete, port or restrict your data, or object to its use, by writing to latitudcerostudio@gmail.com. We reply within 15 days. You can also complain to Ecuador's Superintendencia de Protección de Datos Personales. If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. California residents can also exercise their rights under the CCPA through the same email. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising.

**Children:** this site is not directed at children, and we do not collect their data.
