4. How to order and pay

  1. You fill in the form and receive your proposal by email. The proposal is valid for 15 days.
  2. You accept it by replying "I ACCEPT" from your email, or by signing online.
  3. Initial payment: 50 % to start and 50 % at launch.
  4. Monthly fee: starts on the day your site goes live, with a 12-month commitment, paid monthly in advance.
  5. Payment: PayPal (cards accepted) from abroad, bank transfer in Ecuador. We issue an electronic invoice for every payment, as Ecuadorian law requires.

5. What the monthly fee includes

Hosting or the platform plan (Wix, or hosting for coded sites), renewal of the main domain, and the small changes your plan includes each month. Unused changes do not roll over. Larger changes are quoted before we do them.

6. Changes before launch

Two rounds of design changes are included. A round is one list of changes sent at once. Extra rounds are quoted.
